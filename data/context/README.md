`acs_senior_stop_tracts.csv`: American Community Survey 2020-2024 5-year estimates (tables B01001 age by sex, B08201 vehicles
available), fetched Nov 15, 2026 from the Census Reporter API (api.censusreporter.org), for the census tracts that contain Loop Local
stops. Stops were placed in tracts with the Census Bureau geocoder (geocoding.geo.census.gov, coordinates from the TripShot GTFS).
Tract estimates for small populations carry wide margins of error (listed below).
90% margins of error (Census Reporter, combined for the derived shares): Princeton 65+ ±1.1 points, no car ±2.8; tract 45.02 65+ ±6.5,
no car ±17.7; tract 42.05 65+ ±1.6, no car ±2.7.
